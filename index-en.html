🗺️ MODULE 2: The Geomorphological Trap and CO₂ Failure Physics

1. Physico-chemical properties and LCO₂ behavior in the event of an accident

The decarbonization project involves the storage and handling of liquid carbon dioxide (LCO₂) in two massive horizontal cryogenic tanks with an individual capacity of 3,000 m³ (totaling 6,000 m³). Storage takes place under a high pressure of 16 bar and an extremely low temperature of -29.4 °C.

  • Liquid CO₂ instantly expands approximately 535 times into its gaseous phase.
  • The density of gaseous CO₂ is around 1.98 kg/m³, making it 1.5 times heavier than air.
  • The cold, heavy gas cloud does not rise; instead, it behaves like a liquid—flowing down slopes and filling lower geological depressions.
