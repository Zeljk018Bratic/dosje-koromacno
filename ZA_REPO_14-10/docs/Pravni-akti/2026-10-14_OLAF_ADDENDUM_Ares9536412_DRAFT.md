**Subject: Additional information and questions — file reference Ares(2026)9536412 (project KOdeCO net zero, EU Innovation Fund, grant ID 101133120)**

Dear Sir/Madam,

Further to my notification of 9 October 2026 and my follow-up of 12 October 2026, I respectfully submit the following additional, document-verified information and four questions.

**1. Status of the environmental impact assessment (EIA) procedure in Croatia.**
The EIA procedure for the KOdeCO net zero project was initiated on 6 October 2025 on the application of Holcim (Hrvatska) d.o.o. (file UP/I-351-03/25-08/46). The public consultation was held from 17 August to 16 September 2026 (Ministry information of 10 August 2026, URBROJ 517-04-1-1-26-28). On 28 August 2026 the operator withdrew its application, and on the same date the Ministry suspended the procedure (decision of 28 August 2026, published 1 September 2026, URBROJ 517-04-1-1-26-30). The complete EIA study (801 pages, published 14 August 2026) remains publicly available on the Ministry's website.

**2. Public statements of the operator on the EU grant.**
On 3 September 2026 the operator confirmed in a published interview (Poslovni dnevnik) that the EU Innovation Fund grant of EUR 116.9 million remains in place, with newly agreed deadlines: final investment decision by 30 June 2027 and operation by 31 May 2030; and that parts of the project (port, CO2 storage tanks) are to be relocated following an agreement with the Municipality of Rasa of September 2025.

**3. Co-incineration of waste-derived fuel at the same installation.**
The EIA study (point 1 above) states that the Koromacno cement works holds a waste-management permit for trial operation (recovery operations R1 and R13) for non-hazardous waste code 19 12 10 (file UP/I-351-04/24-09/7, URBROJ 517-04-2-1-25-8, issued 14 March 2025, valid until 14 March 2027), and describes a waste-derived fuel (GIO) feeding system comprising a primary line (4 t/h) and a precalciner line (15 t/h) with an enclosed fuel store. The study reports annual quantities of code 19 12 10 of approximately 20,700-22,100 tonnes for the four reporting years listed. A screening decision of 29 October 2021 (file UP/I-612-07/21-60/63) approved the reconstruction of the installation "with the aim of reducing CO2 emissions through increased energy recovery of waste".

**4. Discrepancy between the public non-technical summary and the full EIA study.**
The non-technical summary made available to the public during the consultation contains no mention of the waste-derived fuel system, the precalciner feeding line, waste code 19 12 10 or the R1/R13 trial permit, all of which are described in the full study. I raise this as a question of completeness of public information (Article 87 of the Croatian Environmental Protection Act; Aarhus Convention), without attributing intent to any party.

**Questions:**
Q1. Was the granting authority (CINEA) informed of the suspension of the EIA procedure and of the planned relocation of project components? Does the grant agreement permit changes of location or scope, and new milestone dates (FID 30 June 2027; operation 31 May 2030), without a formal amendment, and were any amendments concluded?
Q2. Is the co-incineration of waste-derived fuel (code 19 12 10) at the Koromacno installation part of the funded project or of its GHG-calculations and eligibility assumptions under the grant agreement?
Q3. Does the Commission consider the public consultation of 17 August - 16 September 2026 to have met the information requirements of the EIA Directive and the Aarhus Convention, given the discrepancy described in point 4?
Q4. May I receive copies of any amendments to the grant agreement and of correspondence between the beneficiary and CINEA concerning the suspension of the EIA and the relocation, subject to the applicable transparency rules?

I remain at your disposal for any clarification and I request to be informed of the outcome of the assessment.
