## 2. Anchor Evidence — 2023

**FACT · PRIMARY SOURCE**

- **Company:** HOLCIM (CROATIA) Ltd.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Quantity:** 1,457.24 t of hazardous waste subject to the notification procedure
- **EWC/KBO codes:**
  - `12 01 09*` — machining emulsions and solutions containing hazardous substances
  - `13 02 05*` — mineral-based non-chlorinated engine, gear and lubricating oils
  - `13 05 07*` — oily water from oil/water separators
  - `19 02 07*` — oils and concentrates from physico-chemical treatment
- **Operation:** R1 — energy recovery
- **Countries of origin (for the stated category):** Italy and Slovenia
- **Destination:** Koromačno
- **Source:** Ministry of Environment and Green Transition — *Cross-border waste shipment report for 2023*, Table 4.2 / 4.6

**What this record proves:** that in 2023 Holcim processed 1,457.24 t of hazardous waste through the cross-border notification system, with Italy and Slovenia as countries of origin for the stated category, using R1 recovery.

**What this record does not prove:** it does not provide a breakdown of the 1,457.24 t by individual country. Do not claim all 1,457.24 t came from Italy.

---

## 3. Key notes

1. **2023 is the Anchor Evidence** — the only row where a primary source explicitly links Holcim, hazardous waste, EWC codes, Italy and Slovenia, and R1.
2. **2024 must not be equated entirely with Italy** — the source confirms Holcim’s quantity and permit, but not that all 2,350.18 t came from Italy.
3. **Notification number and approval date** — missing for almost all years. This is the main subject of the FOI-04 request to the Ministry / Inspectorate / e-ONTO / DIWASS.
4. **EWC codes with an asterisk (*)** — indicate the waste is classified as hazardous, but this alone does not make the operation illegal. What matters is whether there was a valid legal basis, permit, and cross-border notification.
