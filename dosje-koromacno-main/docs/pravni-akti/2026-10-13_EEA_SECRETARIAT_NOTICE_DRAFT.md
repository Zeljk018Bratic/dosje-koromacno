2. 3. The management of the beneficiary is subject to an ongoing criminal investigation (USKOK, opened 02/2025, expanded 10/2026); one member of the management board was arrested on 09.10.2026 and released pending investigation.
4. The same facts have been notified to OLAF (reference Ares(2026)9536412, 09.10.2026, supplemented 12.–13.10.2026) and to national authorities (DORH/ODO Pula, State Inspectorate).
I respectfully ask the Secretariat / Programme Operator to: (a) confirm whether project monitoring has verified the **location of deployment of grant-funded equipment**; (b) inform me whether any suspension, recovery or eligibility review procedure has been initiated; (c) advise whether further documentation should be submitted through your fraud-reporting channel.
