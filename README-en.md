## 🖥️ How to use this interactive system

This repository was created with the aim of full transparency and arming the citizens of Labinština and Istria with irrefutable engineering and legal facts.

### 1. Running the interactive page live

The complete research dossier, chronology, underwater ecocide calculations and interactive criminal complaint form are active in real time:

> **[OPEN THE KOROMAČNO DOSSIER LIVE](https://zeljk018bratic.github.io/dosje-koromacno/)**

### 2. How to automatically generate a physical leaflet and complaint (Ctrl + P)

The page is programmatically optimised for printing and street distribution:

1. Press **Ctrl + P** (or *Cmd + P* on Mac) or click the red **"Print Leaflet"** button at the top of the page.
2. The system automatically hides all digital navigation buttons, menus and dark backgrounds.
3. A clean, black-and-white legal document and formal criminal complaint form for DORH is sent to the printer – ready for printing, signature and distribution on the ground.

### 3. How to download the source code for your own use

For local use without internet access:

1. Click on the `index.html` file on GitHub.
2. In the top-right corner click **Download raw file**.
3. Save the file to your device and open it in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).

### 4. Legal invocation of the Aarhus Convention and the right of access to information

In accordance with the **Aarhus Convention** (Convention on Access to Information, Public Participation in Decision-making and Access to Justice in Environmental Matters) and the **Croatian Act on the Right of Access to Information (OG 25/13)**, public authorities are obliged to provide citizens and associations with complete, timely and non-erasable insight into all relevant environmental parameters, without the obligation to prove legal interest.

Any denial, manipulation or delayed validation of raw data (RAW DATA) on emissions to air and sea, as well as the concealment of trial operation of the plant that has been running since November 2025, constitutes a direct violation of international and constitutional law.

## 📝 Updated FOIA template

**Subject:** Request for access to information – EWC codes, input analyses, AMS data, weighbridge records and supervision status

> **[Download the full template →](docs/Pravni-akti/dopunjeni-zppi-predlozak.md)**

Dear Sir/Madam,

Pursuant to the Croatian Act on the Right of Access to Information (OG 25/13, 85/15, 69/22), I hereby submit a request for delivery of:

**1. EWC codes and input analyses**
- An exact list of all EWC (catalogue) waste codes taken over or planned to be taken over from the location Bilajska 50, Gospić (big-bag bags) for recovery at Holcim Hrvatska d.o.o. Koromačno.
- For each EWC code: quantity (tonnes), date/time of takeover, results of all input chemical and physical analyses (calorific value, chlorine content, fluorine, heavy metals – especially Hg, Cd, Tl, Sb, Co, As, Pb, Cr, Cu, Mn, Ni, V – moisture, ash, granulometry).
- Confirmation that each listed EWC code is explicitly permitted by the valid waste management permit and environmental permit of Holcim Hrvatska d.o.o.
- A copy of the relevant parts of the valid waste management permit and environmental permit containing the list of permitted EWC codes for the R1 procedure.

**2. Weighbridge records / delivery records**
- All weighbridge records, delivery notes and records of quantities taken over (EWC code, mass, date, time, vehicle registration) from 10 September 2026 to the date of the request.

**3. Raw AMS data**
- Raw data from continuous emission measurement (TOC, HCl, HF, Hg, NOx, SO₂, dust and other continuously measured parameters) from 1 January 2026 to the date of the request, in the form of half-hourly and daily mean values (Excel/CSV).

**4. Air quality supervision status and exceedances**
- A list of parameters that are continuously and periodically measured (including dioxins/furans and heavy metals).
- Whether the data are publicly available in real time and at what address.
- All recorded exceedances of limit values in 2026 (date, time, parameter, value).

Please deliver in electronic form within the statutory deadline.

Yours faithfully,
**[Name and surname]**
**[Address / e-mail]**

**You can send it in parallel to:**
- Ministry of Environment and Green Transition

## 📬 Where to send the completed and signed criminal complaint?

Once you download, fill in and personally sign (or stamp with the association's seal) the criminal complaint template, it must legally be sent to the official addresses:

| Institution | Email | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State Attorney's Office of the Republic of Croatia (DORH)** | `tajnistvo@dorh.hr` | Main address for receiving criminal complaints |
| **MZOZT** | `javnost@mzozt.hr`, `press@mzozt.hr` | Requests for insight and pollution reports |
| **State Inspectorate (DIRH)** | `prijave@dirh.hr` | Urgent action and unannounced inspection |
| **FZOEU** | `kontakt@fzoeu.hr` | Queries related to remediation contracts |
| **ODO Pula-Pola** | `tajnistvo@odopu.dorh.hr` | Criminal complaint – **must state INJURED PARTY status** |
| **USKOK** | `pisarnica@uskok.dorh.hr` | Cc copy of the complaint |
| **Municipality of Raša** | `protokol@rasa.hr` | Requests for environment and plans |
| **City of Labin** | `pisarnica@labin.hr` | Requests for budget and CityX |
| **Istria County Assembly** | `skupstina@istra-istria.hr`, `strucna.sluzba@istra-istria.hr` | Committee for Petitions and Complaints (chairman Dalibor Macan) |
