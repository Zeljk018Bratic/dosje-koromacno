## 7. MEĐUNARODNI WEBINAR – ZERO WASTE EUROPE / GAIA (E-115 do E-118)

**Događaj:** „Beyond Burning: Stories from the Ground“  
**Datum:** 15.10.2026.  
**Organizatori:** Zero Waste Europe + GAIA  
**Kontekst:** Global Week of Action to Stop Waste Incineration (13.–16.10.2026.)

### Ključni sudionici
- **Marija (Maris) Sperac** – predsjednica inicijative „Maksimir za Zagreb“
- Mathilde Rosso – Collective 3R (Francuska)
- Wemi Oku – GAIA Africa (Nigerija)
- Enzo Favoino – Scuola Agraria (Italija)

### Relevantni citati

**Marija Sperac (cca 12:42–13:03):**
> „We have another issue that is closely connected to this one... in Croatia also, the local communities has organized to challenge a proposed **carbon capture project** with an important decision also expected today. Their opposition actually reflects growing concerns about **waste burning in cement plants**. So, we have **two very active cement plants**. They have different permits... and wider permits... and the carbon capture itself... the concern is that it's environmental consequences... where ambition carbon capture have faced serious financial and implementation setbacks.“

> „Are we really building our waste management system around reducing waste or are we building infrastructure that will **need waste to remain economically viable** with all this facilities?“

**Enzo Favoino (cca 20:45) – CCS kao „smoke screen“:**
> „Total cost for the incinerator in Rome will be 1 billion euros... 200 million euros of that will be devoted for implementing a carbon capture technology and to capture 400 tons of carbon dioxide a year... Well, they will emit a 600,000 tons of carbon dioxide a year. So, they will capture only less than 1,000 of the total emission... This is becoming a **smoke screen** to say we align with the agenda on decarbonization.“

### Taktičke lekcije (iz nastupa)
1. Investigate before you protest  
2. Build a coalition of competence  
3. Translate public mobilization into institutional and legal action  
4. Don’t do the Sauron eye (gledaj cijeli sustav)

**Forenzička relevantnost:** Javna međunarodna potvrda veze carbon capture projekata (KOdeCO) sa spaljivanjem otpada u cementarama i ovisnošću infrastrukture o kontinuiranoj opskrbi otpadom.
