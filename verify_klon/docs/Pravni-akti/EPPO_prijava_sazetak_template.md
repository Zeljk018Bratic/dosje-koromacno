**Sažetak (ispuniti kad stignu ZPPI odgovori):**

Poštovani,

prijavljujem sumnju na prekogranična kaznena djela povezana s ilegalnim uvozom i odlaganjem otpada iz Italije u Republiku Hrvatsku (lokacija Bilajska 50, Gospić – Tipos Resurs d.o.o. i povezane osobe), u razdoblju 2022.–2024.

**Ključne činjenice (ažurirati s dokumentima):**

4. Postoje indicije o mogućem sukobu interesa u fazi sanacije (tvrtke koje su ranije poslovale s Tipos Resursom sada sudjeluju u javnim nabavama za uklanjanje istog otpada).

## ENGLISH VERSION (for EPPO web form)

**Subject:**  
Cross-border illegal import and dumping of waste Italy–Croatia (Gospić / Tipos Resurs) – possible offences against the financial interests of the EU and organised crime

**Summary (to be completed once ZPPI replies arrive):**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I hereby report suspicions of cross-border criminal offences related to the illegal import and dumping of waste from Italy into the Republic of Croatia (location Bilajska 50, Gospić – Tipos Resurs d.o.o. and related persons), in the period 2022–2024.

**Key facts (to be updated with documents):**

1. According to investigative documentation, at least **12,338 tonnes** of waste arrived from Italy in **576 shipments** destined for Gospić in the said period.
2. There are indications of misdeclaration of waste (use of Annex VII / green list instead of the notification procedure) and of inconsistency between declared KBO/EWC codes and the actual composition (including hazardous substances and PFAS compounds).
3. One concrete shipment was stopped at the **Pasjak** border crossing; a laboratory analysis was carried out showing a mixture of plastics, metals and elevated concentrations of heavy metals.
4. There are indications of a possible conflict of interest in the remediation phase (companies that previously did business with Tipos Resurs are now participating in public tenders for the removal of the same waste).
5. According to public statements, the Croatian Security and Intelligence Agency (SOA) had information as early as 2022.

**Possible offences falling under EPPO competence:**
- Cross-border fraud / falsification of customs and waste documentation
- Organised crime with a cross-border dimension
- Possible damage to the financial interests of the EU (if VAT fraud, misuse of funds or similar is established)
